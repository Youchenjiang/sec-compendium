## 4. [AI Foundations I - The Lost Book of Perceptrons](https://learn.cylabacademy.org/learning-paths/20)
> A beginner-friendly journey through the lost book of perceptrons. In the year 2030, everyone uses AI, but almost no one remembers how to build it. This path reconstructs the fundamentals by recovering the earliest pages of AI knowledge: how neurons work, how perceptrons are expressed and trained, and why responsible use matters. These challenges emphasize hands-on learning with repetition of core concepts to build a strong foundation.
